FAQ

Clear answers before you start.

What do I need for Sugar Report Clarity?

Use a recent report with the supported values you want explained. Clarity Blood Sugar explains what is provided, identifies uncertainty, and gives questions to discuss with a healthcare professional.

Is every personalised routine reviewed by a dietitian?

Personalised routines are released only after a qualified practitioner reviews and approves them. Your private portal shows the reviewer and approved plan version.

How quickly will I receive support?

Turnaround is confirmed with you before support begins, based on the selected service and current availability. You can enquire on WhatsApp before committing.

What happens to my data?

Your submission requires explicit consent. Use the privacy and data-deletion links in the footer to understand handling and request deletion.
